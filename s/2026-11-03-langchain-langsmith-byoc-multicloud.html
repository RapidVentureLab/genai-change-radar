LangChain expands LangSmith BYOC to Azure and Google Cloud

Teams can keep traces, prompts, datasets and agent deployments in their cloud account while LangChain manages platform operations. Google Cloud data planes can run in any region, but LangSmith manages them from a US control plane.

What to do

Map traces, prompts, secrets and deployment data to the right cloud boundary. Compare BYOC operations and control-plane data with self-hosting before choosing a beta cloud.
